# Build Brief: Make AI Job Search Kit for Women a Sellable Guide

## Recommended builder

Use **Claude Design first**.

This product does not need more code yet. It needs to feel like a polished, premium, useful guide that a woman would happily pay $27 for and not feel scammed by.

Codex should come later for:

- adding the product to the Canopy website
- creating a checkout/download page
- wiring a payment link
- building a gated delivery page
- automating email delivery

Claude Design is better for this stage because the core job is:

- editorial layout
- workbook design
- typography
- page rhythm
- buyer trust
- making prompts and worksheets feel clean, not overwhelming
- exporting a polished PDF-style artifact

## Core assignment

Turn the existing markdown workbook into a polished, sellable PDF guide/workbook called:

**AI Job Search Kit for Women**

Subtitle:

**A practical reset for women who want better work, clearer materials, and a calmer way to use AI in the job search.**

The final guide should feel like a warm, elevated, useful workbook from Canopy, not a generic AI ebook, Canva template, or corporate HR packet.

## Target buyer

The buyer is a woman who:

- wants remote or better work
- feels tired, overwhelmed, or stuck
- may not know how to use AI well
- does not want to sound fake, inflated, or overly corporate
- wants practical help with resume, LinkedIn, applications, outreach, and interviews
- is likely finding this through Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, or a direct referral

She should feel:

- relieved
- guided
- not talked down to
- not overwhelmed by AI language
- like she can open the guide and do the next step immediately

## Product positioning

This is not a giant course.

This is a practical reset kit.

It should feel like:

- a workbook
- a step-by-step guide
- a calm job search command center
- a practical AI prompt collection with context and judgment

It should not feel like:

- a tech manual
- an HR compliance document
- a generic ebook
- a SaaS lead magnet
- a pink Canva worksheet pack
- a fake luxury PDF
- an AI prompt dump

## Visual direction

Follow the Canopy website direction:

- elevated woman-owned work studio
- warm editorial
- practical, human, and trusted
- refined, not girly/coquette
- not AI-tech
- not SaaS
- not neon
- not fake dashboard
- not glassmorphism
- not robot/circuit motifs

Suggested visual language:

- warm ivory / soft cream background
- deep ink text
- muted olive/sage accent
- warm tan or clay accent sparingly
- thin rules
- generous margins
- editorial section headers
- understated numbered steps
- calm worksheet boxes
- subtle paper texture if possible
- simple typographic hierarchy

Avoid:

- bright gradients
- huge rounded SaaS cards everywhere
- excessive icons
- emoji-heavy design
- AI sparkle motifs
- fake metrics
- generic stock photos
- random decorative blobs
- overly feminine pink/coquette styling

## Typography direction

Use an editorial type system.

Recommended structure:

- Serif or elegant display type for cover/title/major section titles
- Clean sans-serif for body text
- Monospace only for prompt blocks, if needed

The guide must be easy to read. Do not sacrifice readability for aesthetics.

Recommended feel:

- title: elegant, calm, confident
- body: practical and legible
- prompts: clear, contained, copyable
- worksheets: spacious enough to write in if printed

## Page structure

Create a polished guide of approximately 14-20 pages.

Recommended structure:

1. Cover page
2. How to use this kit
3. Before you paste anything into AI
4. Part 1: Pick your target lane
5. Role fit worksheet
6. Prompt: find my best-fit roles
7. Part 2: Resume audit
8. Resume audit checklist
9. Prompt: resume positioning audit + bullet rewrite
10. Part 3: LinkedIn refresh
11. LinkedIn headline/About prompts
12. Part 4: Remote job search filters
13. Search phrases + red flags
14. Part 5: Outreach without sounding desperate
15. Outreach templates + customization prompt
16. Part 6: Interview prep
17. STAR worksheet + interview prompt
18. Part 7: Application tracker
19. 7-day reset plan
20. One-page job search brief + final note

If it becomes too long, prioritize usefulness over arbitrary page count.

## Content handling rules

Keep the approved voice:

- direct
- warm
- practical
- clear
- not motivational fluff
- not corporate
- not fake hype

Do not add claims like:

- guaranteed job
- land a remote role in X days
- recruiter-approved unless verified
- six-figure promises
- AI will get you hired

Do not add fake testimonials, fake metrics, or fake logos.

## Design requirements by section

### Cover page

Must include:

- product title: AI Job Search Kit for Women
- subtitle
- Canopy branding
- short promise line: “Use AI to get clearer, apply smarter, and stop rebuilding everything from scratch.”
- elegant, minimal layout

Optional small footer:

“Canopy | Practical AI systems for work and life”

### How to use this kit

Make this page feel immediately useful.

Use a 90-minute timeline layout:

- 15 minutes: choose target roles
- 20 minutes: audit resume
- 20 minutes: refresh LinkedIn
- 15 minutes: build remote filters
- 10 minutes: create outreach
- 10 minutes: prep interview stories

### Prompt blocks

Prompt blocks should be visually distinct and easy to copy.

Design treatment:

- light cream/tan background
- thin border
- small label: “Copy/paste prompt”
- monospace or clearly differentiated text
- enough padding
- no tiny text

### Worksheets

Worksheet fields should feel printable and fillable.

Use:

- clean lines
- labeled boxes
- enough blank space
- no cramped tables

### Checklists

Checklists should use clean square boxes, not decorative icons.

### 7-day reset plan

Make this one of the most visually useful pages.

Use a simple day-by-day structure:

- Day 1: choose target roles
- Day 2: resume audit
- Day 3: LinkedIn refresh
- Day 4: job search filters
- Day 5: apply intentionally
- Day 6: outreach
- Day 7: interview prep

### One-page job search brief

This should feel like a command center page.

It should be printable and easy to use as the buyer’s central job search sheet.
