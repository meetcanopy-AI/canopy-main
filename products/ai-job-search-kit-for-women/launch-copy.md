## Facebook group value post

A lot of women are not struggling because they have “nothing to offer.”

They’re struggling because their job search materials are trying to say too many things at once.

Before you rewrite your resume again, try this:

Pick **2 primary roles** and **1 backup role**.

Then ask:

1. Does my resume make those roles obvious within 10 seconds?
2. Does my LinkedIn headline match those roles?
3. Do my bullets show the kind of work those roles need?
4. Am I using the same keywords recruiters are searching?
5. Am I applying with a clear lane or panic-applying to everything?

AI can help with this, but only if you give it real context. Don’t ask it to “make my resume better.” Ask it to position you for a specific role.
