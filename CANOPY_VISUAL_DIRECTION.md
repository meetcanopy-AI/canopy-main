# Canopy Visual Direction

## North Star
Canopy should feel like an elevated woman-owned work studio: calm, editorial, useful, and quietly premium. It should look human and trusted before it looks technical.

## What it is
- Woman-owned, but not girly or coquette
- Practical, warm, refined
- HR/operator credibility with founder-led taste
- A clean place to buy guides, join the job-search beta, or book a business audit
- More field guide / private office / editorial desk than AI startup

## What it is not
- Not AI slop
- Not SaaS template
- Not neon gradients, cyber grids, glassmorphism, fake dashboards, robot icons, holograms, circuit lines, glowing blobs, or “unlock your potential” filler
- Not pink-purple-blue AI palette
- Not over-explaining AI like a tech company
- Not childish, overly feminine, or coquette

## Visual System
- Background: warm ivory, parchment, ink, espresso, olive, muted clay
- Typography: elegant serif for big editorial moments, restrained sans for body and UI
- Layout: editorial homepage with clear offer blocks, not repeated generic card grids
- Texture: subtle paper/grain, thin rules, small labels, quiet spacing
- Imagery: real founder photo used as trust/proof; avoid stock photos and fake product imagery
- Buttons: simple, tactile, high-contrast; no glow effects
- Motion: minimal. Hover lift only if needed.

## Copy Rules
- Say “AI” only when useful.
- Use normal-person language.
- Avoid AI-site phrases: transform, unlock, revolutionize, harness the power, supercharge, seamless, cutting-edge, next-gen, empower, elevate your workflow.
- Be specific about outcomes: resume bullets, LinkedIn cleanup, interview prep, remote role positioning, SOPs, hiring workflows, admin systems.

## Conversion Rules
- Every page section must route to one of three buyer paths:
  1. Job seeker / remote work help
  2. Quick Guides / prompting help
  3. Business systems audit
- No fake proof, fake metrics, fake testimonials, or invented client logos.
- Temporary mailto CTAs are acceptable until checkout/forms are live.
