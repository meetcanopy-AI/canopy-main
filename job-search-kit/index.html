FAQ

Plain answers before you buy.

Do I need to be good with AI?

No. The prompts are written so you can copy, paste, and fill in your own context. The point is to make AI useful without needing to become a tech person.

Is this only for remote jobs?

No. It helps with any better-work search, but it includes remote-specific search language, filters, and red flags because that is what many buyers are looking for.

Will this write my whole resume for me?

It helps you audit and rewrite stronger sections, but it does not replace your judgment. It is designed to keep your materials truthful and human.
